Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

TikTok Manager is designed to manage authorized TikTok account workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how information handled by the application is used.

Information handled

When a user authorizes the application, TikTok Manager may receive information made available through TikTok APIs and the permissions approved by the user, such as account identifiers and follower statistics. The application may also process video files and scheduling information provided by the user.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account management, scheduling, publishing, and monitoring functions of the application.

Sharing

Information is not sold. Information obtained through TikTok APIs is not shared with unrelated third parties except when necessary to provide an authorized integration or when required by law.

Security and retention

Access credentials and authorization tokens should be stored securely. Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functions or comply with applicable legal requirements.

Deletion

Users may request deletion of information handled by the application through the contact method associated with the application. Information will be deleted when required, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Third-party services

TikTok is a separate service. Information processed directly by TikTok is governed by TikTok's own privacy policies.
